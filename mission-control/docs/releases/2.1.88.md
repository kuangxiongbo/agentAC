# 2.1.88 - Offline Human Watch Auto-stop

Date: 2026-07-29

## Changes

- Enforces Human Watch binding auto-stop limits before checking Worker session or Edge Bridge availability.
- Ensures an offline Edge cannot keep an expired binding enabled beyond `max_runtime_seconds`.
- Stops the poll before transcript fetch, steward judge or reliable-message creation after a limit is reached.

## Validation

- Focused Human Watch regression: 22 tests passed.
- Full Center suite: 1186 tests passed.
- Center typecheck, focused lint and production build passed.
- A 120-second offline binding is the production gray-release acceptance gate.

## Upgrade And Rollback

- Upgrade Center and Edge Runtime to 2.1.88. No database migration; Tray remains 3.0.10.
- Rollback to 2.1.87 is data-compatible. Existing bindings, events and mailbox records are unchanged.

## Gray Risk

- Auto-stop is evaluated by the Center poll interval, so disabling may occur up to one poll interval after the configured deadline.
